### 1. Fresh-spawn ambush: hostile Meridian Frigate spawns over starter camp on Day 1 and damages player before spotting toast
- **Evidence:** `06:53:41` ("Oh, okay, hold on"), `06:53:46` ("I just spawned in with a f*cking frigate shooting at me"); frames `vn-20261120-065341-759-0.5.jpg` (starter bonus chest open with the frigate hovering ~70 m behind it), `065341-759-0.8.jpg` (health already 11/20 as "A Meridian Frigate looms on the horizon" appears), `065346-719-0.8.jpg` (blast crater right beside spawn). Metadata: `-6s entered Surface` → `-4s A Meridian Frigate looms on the horizon; took 9 damage (11/20)` → `-2s The Meridian Frigate's crew has spotted you!`.
- **Root cause:**
  - `Sources/ShipVessels.swift:222,233,273-286`: `Vessels.out("frigate", fleetSails: false)` is `true` from minute 0, and `Vessels.encounter` hashes a Meridian Frigate (`h < 0.06`) anywhere in the 2048-block region with **no minimum distance from world spawn** (`encounterTick` spawns it as soon as `d <= 520`).
  - `Sources/CapitalShips.swift:1685,1752-1756,3024-3066`: `capitalGuns` has no post-spawn or post-spotting grace window; turrets can fire on the very first tick the player is targeted while the spotting toast/sound is still firing or queued.
- **Recommended fix:**
  1. Exclude `Vessels.encounter` hostile warships (`frigate`, `warfrigate`, `crawler`, `battle`) within **600–800 blocks of world spawn** (push `home` out or return `nil` inside the safe radius).
  2. Add a 4–5 s warning grace timer after `st.engaged` flips `true` (and after world load) before capital turrets/missiles open fire on a Survival player so `"spotted you!"` + horn actually warn the player before the first shell lands.
- **Risk:** Very low (players hunting frigates still find them one region out; fresh spawns stop getting insta-bombed at the starter chest).

### 2. Render distance drops to 6–8 and won't climb back (plus unmentioned 67–69 fps dips even at view 6)
- **Evidence:** `06:54:39` ("Definitely better at 12"), `06:56:50`–`06:57:14` (drops to 6 over Greywall battle, stays stuck at 6 after flying away: "That's six. It should immediately update to twelve"), `06:57:19` (manual 22), `06:58:33` ("What? The render distance is at 8 now? What the hell?"), `06:58:42`; frames `065650-0.8`, `065714-0.5/0.8`, `065833-0.5/0.8`. Metadata shows FPS dipping to **69** (`06:56:04`) and **67** (`06:56:08`, `06:57:07`, `06:57:10`, `06:57:14`) even after flying away at view 6.
- **Root cause:**
  - Build `3376e5ab` lacked PR #823 (Ledger 382's `lidLeave` + 2-second quad spike grace + 100% `raiseRoom`), so the 10-minute `lid` and 1-second `overQuads` spike locked him at 6.
  - **Still broken on `origin/claude/quest-port` even with Ledger 382:**
    1. **Flying freezes raises (`Sources/ViewGovernor.swift:226-229`, `quest/src/app/QuestApp.swift:1149`):** `if w.excused || w.loading || sinceChange < Self.settle { return .none }` skips `good += w.seconds` whenever `genRate > FrameStats.loadingChunksPerSecond`. Flying away from a heavy area keeps `w.loading == true`, so the climb clock never advances.
    2. **Still 75+ s minimum to climb 6→12 (`ViewGovernor.swift:44,70,226,246,256-258`):** Each 2-chunk step pays `settle = 8 s` (consumes two 5 s windows = 10 s) + `keepHold = 15 s` = **25 s per step** (75 s for 6→8→10→12), and **any window with >1% missed frames (`w.missed * 100 > w.frames`, `:246`) resets `good = 0`** (67 fps ≈ 7% missed → never climbs).
    3. **Asymmetric drop/raise math (`ViewGovernor.swift:168,244`):** `overQuads` drops using `pow(..., 0.8)` and `quadFloor = 6` (`:81`), slamming 22 → 8 in one step (`06:58:33`) or to 6 during frigate/fortress fights; raises predict quads with `exponent = 1.2` (`122k * (8/6)^1.2 = 172k > 170k`), trapping wooded biomes below 12.
    4. **`CostProbe` self-inflicted dips (`quest/src/app/QuestApp.swift:1011-1013`):** Still armed in store/playtest builds on any slow 5 s window, doubling sky+terrain draws for 48 frames right when the player is already hitting 67 fps.
- **Recommended fix:**
  1. Below `keepView` (12), allow `good` to accumulate during normal flight streaming (`w.loading`) when `gpuMs` has headroom, cut `settle` to `3 s` and `keepHold` to `5–8 s` (15–24 s for 6→12), and allow raising 2 steps at once when `current <= 8` and `quads`/`gpuMs` clearly fit 12.
  2. Raise `quadFloor` from `6` to `8` (matches PauseMenu help text `"8 to 24 chunks"` visible in `065833-0.8`), match raise `exponent` to `0.85–0.9` (headset-measured curve), and don't let `overQuads` drop below 12 on a 2-second burst if GPU ms is under budget (keep GPU ms authoritative over quad estimates).
  3. Disable `CostProbe` by default (opt-in `BS_COSTPROBE=1`), and apply the shader/FFR/LOD cuts from `docs/status/render-distance-review.md` so 12 holds 72 fps without dipping to 67.
- **Risk:** Low–medium; validate on `tools/headset_gate.sh --route all` to confirm no <60 fps oscillation.

### 3. Tree-felling & fallen-log system: 4 distinct bugs hit in one 75-second Taiga walk
- **Evidence:** `06:58:50` ("Both logs fell together in one hit"), `06:59:20`–`06:59:35` ("little flash that happens when I hit a log. It's almost like it despawns this normal tree then respawns the one that falls... doesn't happen all the time"), `06:59:40` ("This one caused both logs to fall"), `06:59:52` ("I just hit a log and it disappeared. Log already on the ground. And it turned into a way bigger log"), `07:00:03` ("And it's rolling as if the top and bottom are connected"). Frames: `065850-0.5/0.8` and `065940-0.5` (two adjacent 1×1 spruce trunks), `065952-0.5` (middle of ground log gone), `065952-0.8` & `070003-0.8` (two fat separated log halves rolling rigidly as one body with a 1-block air gap).
- **Root causes:**
  1. **Ground log grows ~2× thicker & rolls as two invisibly connected halves (`Sources/Felling.swift:321-459,666-690`, `Sources/ShapeTrunks.swift:906,918-919`):** Breaking the middle block of a world-generated horizontal fallen log invokes `fellWhole(p, cut)`. Because horizontal logs have no upright `feet` (`axisOf == 1`), `trunks == 0` (`:360`), so `fellWhole` skips the trunk split (`:398`) and passes **both disconnected halves** (`comp`, missing `p`) into one `Blueprint` / single `Ship` in `fellLoose` — which never calls `splitIfNeeded(s)`. Simultaneously, `FellShape.shapeBuild` remeshes the two shortened pieces via `ShapeTrunks.timber` (`:918`), where `fallenRun(ctx, x, y, z)` requires ≥3 contiguous logs; each 2-block half fails `fallenRun` and falls back to built-timber `r = 0.46` instead of `fallenRadius = 0.26` (**77% wider / 3.1× area**).
  2. **Two separate trees fall in one hit (`Sources/Felling.swift:365,427-438`):** `for g in feet where label[g] == nil && abs(g.x - c.x) <= 1 && abs(g.z - c.z) <= 1 && abs(g.y - c.y) <= 2` merges any two trunks standing 1 block apart (or diagonally adjacent) into the same trunk label (intended only for 2×2 mega trunks), and `slab` (`:427-438`) then cuts across both trunks.
  3. **Intermittent disappear-and-pop flash when chopping a tree (`Sources/Felling.swift:679-698,865-881`, `Sources/FellShape.swift:121-128`):** `fellLoose` removes the standing blocks (`setBlockAsync`) and hides the falling `Ship` (`s.hidden = true`) until **both** chunk remesh (`drawn`, `:865`) **and** `s.mesh` + async `Felling.shapePending` (`blocksmith.fellshape` queue, `:876`) finish. Whenever the chunk worker removes the standing tree *before* `shapeQueue` / `ships.meshQueue` finish, the tree disappears completely for 2–15 frames before reappearing as the falling body. When `shapeQueue` wins the race, there is no flash.
- **Recommended fix:**
  1. In `fellWhole` (`Felling.swift:358`), if `trunks == 0` (horizontal fallen log / loose wood), **do not fell as one whole body** (either break just the struck log cleanly, or gather the fallen log whole; and if `fellLoose` ever spawns disconnected cells, run `world.ships.splitIfNeeded(s)` and preserve `fallenRadius` for horizontal logs in `ShapeTrunks.timber` when `ghosts` contains the cut log).
  2. Restrict `Felling.swift:365` multi-foot merging to genuine 2×2 trunks (4 feet forming a 2×2 square of the same wide species, not two 1×1 spruces touching diagonally or sharing crowns).
  3. Eliminate the flash by **deferring `setBlockAsync(..., AIR)` until `s.mesh` and `Felling.shapeBuild` are ready** (build the ship mesh & `FellShape` first while the standing tree stays visible, then swap in one `editBatch` / sync remesh the same frame `s.hidden = false`).
- **Risk:** Low–medium; cover all 3 cases in `TreeProbeCheck` (adjacent 1×1 spruces, horizontal fallen log cut in middle, zero-gap frame swap).

### 7. Music isn't cycling — repeats the same track over and over
- **Evidence:** `07:02:04` ("The music is not cycling that well. It's kind of just the same song over and over again"), `07:02:11` ("We have plenty of songs in the actual script, so").
- **Root cause:** Two compounding bugs in `Sources/Soundtrack.swift`:
  1. **Creative mode hijacks the first 10 minutes (`Soundtrack.swift:159`):** `if !survival && Int(clock / 600) % 3 == 0 { return "creative_mode" }` overrides *every* biome (Plains, Taiga, Frozen Peaks, Cold Ocean, Beach) for the entire first 10 minutes (`clock` 0–600 s) of Creative play, restricting playback to just 3 tracks (`creative_mode`, `_2`, `_3`, `SoundtrackGains.swift:35-37`).
  2. **Broken shuffle history for 3-take states (`Soundtrack.swift:92-104`):** `let keep = min(4, all.count / 2)` gives **`keep = 1`** whenever `all.count == 3` (which is true for `"creative_mode"`, `"snow"`, `"old_growth_forest"`, and almost every biome!). After playing A then B, `heard` drops A (`heard.count (2) > 1`), leaving `fresh = [A, C]` → **50% chance to immediately replay A** (A→B→A→B ping-pong).
- **Recommended fix:**
  1. Use a true shuffle-bag (deck) per state in `SoundtrackDirector.nextTake`: keep `all.count - 1` recent entries (for 3 takes, `keep = 2` so all 3 must play before any repeats, and never repeat the last track across bag reshuffles).
  2. In Creative mode (`:159`), don't lock a 10-minute solid block to `"creative_mode"`; either interleave `"creative_mode"` as 1-in-3 tracks alongside the current biome/daytime pool or expand `"creative_mode"`'s pool to include `"overworld_daytime"` takes so players hear the full soundtrack immediately.
- **Risk:** Very low.

- **Root cause:** `Sources/Mesher.swift:1602-1615` meshes `sea_ice` (`sheetT`) and shallow water cells as rigid 16×16 axis-aligned squares (`CT[ci] * 16`) without clipping or fading against the smooth `TerrainField` beach slope, and worldgen leaves 1-block isolated shallow water/`sea_ice` pockets on sloped sand beaches.
- **Recommended fix:** In `WorldGen`/shoreline ice pass, suppress isolated 1–2 block `sea_ice` pockets whose neighbors are mostly land, and extend water/sheet-ice edge vertices slightly under adjacent smooth sloped shore cells (or slope/fade shore water alpha near field terrain intersection) so rectangular tile corners don't poke out of sloped sand.
- **Risk:** Low–medium (verify no underwater seams or z-fighting in beach/ice snapshots).
