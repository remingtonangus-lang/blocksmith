# Sunderfall Quest 3 Open Code Review

Scope: Everything outside render distance (covered in `docs/status/render-distance-review.md`). Code review only against HEAD (`c394535`), cross-checked against `docs/status/bug-ledger.md` and `docs/playtests/2026-11-17/voice-notes.md`. Findings are ranked by value to player experience, stability, Meta Store standing, and Early Access reviews.

---

## Findings Ranked by Value

### 1. Vulkan concurrency violation on launch / world switch (random Adreno crash)
- **Evidence:** `QuestApp.load()` runs on the background thread `blocksmith.load` and calls `scene.prepareCloudMap()` (`quest/src/app/QuestApp.swift:225`, `quest/src/vk/SceneRenderer.swift:451-456`). `CloudMap.init` (`quest/src/vk/CloudMap.swift:101-117`) calls `ctx.oneShot(...)` (`quest/src/vk/Vk.swift:181-204`), which allocates/frees a command buffer from the **shared** `ctx.pool`, calls `vkQueueSubmit(ctx.queue, ...)`, and calls `vkQueueWaitIdle(ctx.queue)` with **no synchronization** while the main thread is rendering the loading scene at 72 Hz on the exact same `ctx.pool` and `ctx.queue` (`SceneRenderer.swift:858,1007-1015,1103`). Ledger 261 added `buildLock` for `modules` and `descPool` (`SceneRenderer.swift:347,448`) but missed `ctx.pool` and `ctx.queue`, violating Vulkan's external-synchronization rules (`VUID-vkAllocateCommandBuffers-commandPool-00043`, `VUID-vkQueueSubmit-queue-00062`).
- **Recommended change:** Remove `ctx.oneShot` from `CloudMap.init`; either run the initial layout transition/clear on the frame thread during `adoptLoaded()` before the first `recordCloudMap()`, or give `CloudMap` a `needsClear` flag cleared at the start of its first `record()` on the frame's command buffer.
- **Expected effect:** Eliminates a driver-level race/crash inside `libvulkan_adreno.so` during cold launch and world switches, and removes a mid-load `vkQueueWaitIdle` stall on the loading screen.
- **Risk:** Very low (one-time clear moves into the existing command buffer before first sample).

### 2. Double-world memory retention on world switch + ignored `APP_CMD_LOW_MEMORY` (OOM crash bomb)
- **Evidence:**
  1. `QuestApp.startLoading` (`quest/src/app/QuestApp.swift:131-183`) captures `let old = savingGame` inside the `Thread` closure (`loadThread`). After saving `old`, the closure keeps `old` strongly retained throughout `self?.load(req)` **and** `Foundation.Thread` keeps its closure alive until `loadThread` is replaced on the *next* world switch (confirmed by Ledger 262's own note at `QuestApp.swift:171-172`). Thus switching worlds once pins the **entire previous `Game`, `World`, chunks, `MeshSlice`s (blocking `MeshArena` slab reuse), mobs, ships, and `SoundEngine` (up to 64 MB audio clips + two live `MusicStream` timers)** in RAM for the whole next session.
  2. `APP_CMD_LOW_MEMORY` (`quest/src/android/AndroidMain.swift:78-79`) only prints `"android: low memory"` and frees nothing.
  3. `MeshArena.tints.trim()` (`Sources/MeshArena.swift:30,244`) is never called anywhere (and `SceneRenderer.tintSets` at `SceneRenderer.swift:941-956` caches `VkDescriptorSet`s forever by `ObjectIdentifier`, which would alias dangling `VkBuffer`s if tint slabs are freed without eviction).
- **Recommended change:** (a) Capture `var old = savingGame` in `startLoading`, set `old = nil` right after saving (followed by `MeshArena.shared.trim()`), and clear `loadThread = nil` once `adoptLoaded()` finishes. (b) On `APP_CMD_LOW_MEMORY`, evict non-playing audio clips, trim `MeshArena.shared`, and drop distant non-dirty chunk buffers/shells. (c) If trimming `MeshArena.tints`, key `tintSets` by a monotonic buffer ID or evict on `QuestBuffer.deinit`.
- **Expected effect:** Cuts post-switch resident RAM roughly in half (hundreds of MB to >1 GB saved) and prevents Android LMK silent kills on 8 GB Quest 3 during long sessions.
- **Risk:** Low; dropping `old` on `blocksmith.load` keeps world teardown off the frame thread (preserving Ledger 262's frame-time fix).

### 3. Production `CostProbe` doubles GPU work during slow frames + 55 sync sysfs reads every 5 s on the frame thread
- **Evidence:**
  1. `CostProbe.on = Env.all["BS_COSTPROBE"] != "0"` (`quest/src/common/CostProbe.swift:15`) is **ON in `STORE=1` builds**. Whenever a player hits one slow 5-second window (`>15%` missed frames, `QuestApp.swift:1010-1012`), `CostProbe.arm()` runs a 120-frame trial that **draws sky+clouds twice** (`SceneRenderer.swift:1176,1189,1198`) for 24 frames and **draws all opaque terrain twice** (`WorldRenderer.swift:381`) for 24 frames — solely to print a logcat line. Worse, those artificial GPU spikes feed straight into `DynamicResolution.observe` and `ViewGovernor.observe`, tanking eye resolution/view distance and causing VRC.Quest.Performance.1 dips (<60 fps) right when the scene is already heavy.
  2. Every 5 s on the main VR frame thread (`QuestApp.swift:932-937,1076,1092`), `FrameStats.residentMB()` reads `/proc/self/status` and `DeviceClocks.line()` (`quest/src/common/DeviceClocks.swift:8-36`) synchronously opens/reads up to ~55 sysfs files (including up to 40 `/sys/class/thermal/thermal_zoneN/temp` kernel sensor nodes) purely to write to logcat/`blocksmith.log`.
- **Recommended change:** Disable `CostProbe` by default in `STORE=1` builds (`#if VOICE_NOTES` or opt-in `BS_COSTPROBE=1`), and skip `DeviceClocks.line()` / `/proc/self/status` in store builds (or move them to a background queue).
- **Expected effect:** Eliminates self-inflicted 2× GPU spikes during heavy scenes and removes periodic 5-second thermal-sysfs stalls from the VR frame thread.
- **Risk:** None for players; dev/gate runs can set `BS_COSTPROBE=1` when profiling.

### 4. Comfort-mode Teleport locomotion is broken on flat ground and on Polygons (default) terrain
- **Evidence:** `QuestControls.computeArc` (`quest/src/app/QuestControls.swift:1150-1202`) powers the **Comfort** preset advertised in First-Launch Setup (`QuestOptions.swift:69,338`) and `docs/store/comfort-rating.md`:
  1. **Block-center distance bug (`:1171-1172,1189`):** Computes `t` to the hit block's **center** `(h.hit + 0.5)` instead of the ray's surface entry distance. With arc speed 11 and `step = 0.035` (`len ≈ 0.385 m`), a 45° downward hit on a block's top face has its center `~0.35 m` deeper, so `t > len` for >90% of hits. Line `1189` (`if best <= len`) rejects the hit, the next step starts *inside* the solid block, and `raycast` returns `normal == (0,0,0)` → **red arc / teleport refused even on flat grass**.
  2. **Polygons smooth terrain broken (`:1159-1177` vs `Sources/World.swift:2948-2959`):** `standable()` checks only `Blocks.collide` (ignoring `fieldDensity`), `landing` snaps feet to integer `Float(h.hit.y + 1) + 0.01` instead of `h.at` / `w.groundTop` (dropping players from mid-air or burying them in slopes with black `wallFade`), and slopes steeper than 45° return a horizontal `nrm` (`World.swift:2956-2957`), failing `h.normal == IVec3(0, 1, 0)`.
  3. **No body-clearance check (`:1135-1143`):** Teleports directly to `feet` without testing `player.collides(at: feet, w)`, allowing clipping into adjacent walls/overhangs.
- **Recommended change:** Use the true surface hit `t` (`w.fieldHit?.t` or exact `World.rayBox` entry `t`); on field terrain accept walkable surface normals (`-simd_normalize(w.fieldGradient(at)).y > 0.55`) and place feet on `h.at.y` / `w.groundTop`; validate landing with `!game.player.collides(at: feet, w)`.
- **Expected effect:** Turns Comfort-mode teleport from broken/unusable into reliable locomotion across Polygons hills and buildings — critical for motion-sensitive Quest buyers who leave 1★ reviews when Comfort mode fails.
- **Risk:** Low; extend `QuestSim.swift:436` teleport tests to cover Polygons slopes, flat grass, and overhangs.

### 5. VR text input broken when laser-clicking text fields (Creative search, Crafting Book, Anvil, Books)
- **Evidence:** In VR menus, players point with the laser and pull Right Trigger (`QuestControls.swift:1722` → `input.leftClicked = true`). `MenuInput.swift:133-143` calls `m.click(...)` **without** opening `KeyboardMenu` when `m.capturesText` transitions `false → true` — that auto-open (`MenuInput.swift:193-201`) **only runs on button `A` (`p.a && !q.a`)**. Meanwhile `QuestControls.swift:349` → `AndroidMain.swift:126-131` calls `ANativeActivity_showSoftInput`, which is unreliable/unsupported for Horizon OS pure-OpenXR `NativeActivity` apps, and `AndroidMain.swift:116-123` ignores `AKEY_EVENT_ACTION_MULTIPLE` (how Android IMEs deliver composed strings). Unless a Quest user guesses to press `Y` (`MenuInput.swift:302`), laser-clicking Creative Search, Crafting Book Search, Anvil Rename, or Book editing leaves a dead text box.
- **Recommended change:** Mirror `MenuInput.swift:193-201` in the `input.leftClicked` path (`MenuInput.swift:138`) when `vrHead != nil || Prompt.pad` so clicking any text box opens `KeyboardMenu` immediately. Test `ANativeActivity_showSoftInput` on Quest 3; if non-functional, rely exclusively on `KeyboardMenu` and remove the dead OS-keyboard hook.
- **Expected effect:** Unbreaks Creative search, Crafting Book search, Anvil naming, and Books for every VR laser user.
- **Risk:** Very low (keep `!(m is PauseMenu)` so PauseMenu's own `KeyboardMenu` openings aren't duplicated).

### 6. Audio: 44.1 kHz vs Quest 3 48 kHz AAudio mismatch, permanent mute on reopen failure, real-time data races, idle 33 Hz timers
- **Evidence:**
  1. **Sample-rate mismatch:** `Sources/Sound.swift:383` hardcodes `SoundBank.rate = 44100`. `QuestAudioOutput.swift:32-33,55-57` requests `44100` with `AAUDIO_PERFORMANCE_MODE_LOW_LATENCY`, reads `sr = AAudioStream_getSampleRate(st)`, prints it, and **never uses `sr`**. Quest 3 low-latency MMAP hardware runs natively at **48000 Hz**: AAudio either opens at 48 kHz (playing all SFX, voices, and `QuestTrackDeck` music **+8.84% sharp/fast**, since `QuestTracks.swift:41,119` resamples to `44100`) or drops off the low-latency path into software resampling (higher latency and xrun risk).
  2. **Permanent mute on reopen failure:** `QuestAudioOutput.swift:62-71` clears `QuestAudioOutput.lost = false` *before* calling `start(e)`. If `AAudioStreamBuilder_openStream` fails once (common during Bluetooth earbud connect/disconnect or audio-server restart), `stream` stays `nil`, `lost` is `false`, and audio stays **permanently muted** until restart.
  3. **Real-time thread data races & lock contention:** `QuestReverb.setRoom` (`QuestSound.swift:156-158,502-504`) mutates `reverb.feedback` unlocked while `renderBlock` reads it on the AAudio thread; `underwater` and `cave` (`:126-127`) are written outside `mixLock` and read inside `mixLock` (`:416-417`); `QuestAudioOutput.lost`, `level`, and `engine` (`QuestAudioOutput.swift:14,18,19,36,43`) have no synchronization, and the AAudio error callback calls `print(...)` (blocking pipe write).
  4. **Idle background spin:** `QuestSound.swift:113-114,551-597` starts both `music` and `disc` `MusicStream.pump()` loops at launch; they wake every 30 ms (~66 wakeups/sec combined) forever even when no disc or procedural score is playing (e.g. while the Sunderfall `.m4a` soundtrack plays).
- **Recommended change:** (a) Open AAudio at the device's native rate (or 48000 Hz) and pass actual `sr` into `SoundEngine` (or scale mixer playback rate by `SoundEngine.rate / Double(sr)` and resample `QuestTrackDeck` to `sr`). (b) In `watch(now:)`, retry whenever `(lost || stream == nil) && engine != nil` every 1–2 s and only clear `lost` if `stream != nil`. (c) Guard `underwater`/`cave`/`reverb.feedback` under `mixLock` (or atomics), synchronize `engine` swap, and drop `print` from the AAudio callback. (d) Stop `MusicStream.pump()` when `current == nil && rendererFinished` and restart it in `play()`.
- **Expected effect:** Eliminates permanent mute on Bluetooth/headphone switches, guarantees accurate pitch & low-latency audio on Quest 3, removes audio-thread UB/races, and cuts idle wakeups/battery drain.
- **Risk:** Low; scaling playback rate by `44100 / sr` avoids changing `SoundBank.rate` or sound assets.

### 7. Meta VRC entitlement-denial race, triple synchronous pause saves, and signal-handler deadlock
- **Evidence:**
  1. **Entitlement denial race & invisible message (`QuestApp.swift:500-509`, `QuestEntitlement.swift:13-59`):** When `QuestEntitlement.denied` turns `true`, `QuestApp` calls `game?.onToast?(...)` and immediately `switchWorld(WorldRequest())`. If denial arrives during the first 1–3 s while `loadThread` is still loading (`game == nil`), `game?.onToast` is a no-op **and** `switchWorld` spawns a second `blocksmith.load` thread concurrently with the first, exiting immediately with no message. If denial arrives after `game != nil`, `switchWorld` clears `game = nil` on the same frame, destroying the toast before it ever renders. Also `QuestEntitlement.swift:54` treats *any* `ovr_Message_IsError` as `denied = true` (contradicting its comment at `:13` that offline lets play continue), while `ovr_PlatformInitializeAndroid` returning `rc != 0` (`:44`) fails silently open.
  2. **Triple synchronous save + forced menu close on pause (`AndroidMain.swift:71-81`, `QuestApp.swift:493-497,693-702`):** `APP_CMD_PAUSE`, `APP_CMD_SAVE_STATE`, and `XR_SESSION_STATE_STOPPING` each call synchronous `saveNow() + SaveIO.flush()` on the main thread (up to 3 full flushes back-to-back, risking Android's 5 s ANR kill on large worlds), and `APP_CMD_PAUSE` invokes `closeMenusForExit()` (`QuestApp.swift:698`), silently closing any open crafting/chest/sign/report menu when the player just taps the Meta button.
  3. **Unsafe shutdown & crash handler:** `XRSession` has no `deinit` (contradicting `AndroidMain.swift:260`) and `AndroidMain.swift:265` calls `exit(0)` immediately after `ANativeActivity_finish` (skipping `onDestroy` / `BugNotes.shutdown()`). `CrashGuard.onSignal` (`Sources/CrashGuard.swift:57-66`) allocates a Swift `[UInt8]` on the heap inside a `SIGSEGV`/`SIGABRT` handler — async-signal-unsafe, risking a frozen headset deadlock instead of a clean exit and Android tombstone.
- **Recommended change:** (a) On `QuestEntitlement.denied`, stop/avoid spawning a second `loadThread` and show the denial text on the loading scene for 3–4 s (or an explicit Exit screen) before `xr.requestExit()`. (b) Track `savedSinceTick` to deduplicate back-to-back pause/save-state/stopping saves, and only close menus on real exit/world-switch (not transient Meta-button pauses). (c) Replace `Array(...)` in `CrashGuard.onSignal` with a stack-only fixed C buffer / byte tuple. (d) Add `XRSession.deinit` cleanup.
- **Expected effect:** Cleaner VRC.Quest.Security.1 & lifecycle compliance, avoids pause ANRs and unexpected menu closures, and prevents crash-handler deadlocks.
- **Risk:** Low; offline entitlement behavior must be verified on a store-signed APK on Quest 3 in Airplane Mode.

### 8. Per-frame HUD offscreen pass stalls GPU pipeline across frames + force-unwrap crash on empty HUD
- **Evidence:**
  1. `QuestControls.recordPanel` (`quest/src/app/QuestControls.swift:1746-1752`) calls `hudHost.buildHUD(1024, 640)` and `panel.record(s, verts)` **every frame at 72 Hz** — even when `game.hideHUD == true` (`QuestControls.swift:2156` only skips drawing it, not building/rasterizing it) and even when nothing on the HUD changed.
  2. `HudPanel` (`quest/src/vk/HudPanel.swift:10,44-49,94-96,125-158`) double-buffers `vbufs` but uses **one** `image` across 2 frames in flight, with a `VK_SUBPASS_EXTERNAL` (`FRAGMENT_SHADER → COLOR_ATTACHMENT_OUTPUT`) dependency. On Adreno 740, Frame N+1's HUD pass must wait for Frame N's full stereo world pass to finish fragment shading before clearing `image`, serializing frames in flight and paying a 1024×640 RGBA8 (2.6 MB) GMEM clear/store every frame.
  3. **Crash bug (`HudPanel.swift:128-129`):** `verts.withUnsafeBytes { memcpy(vb.mapped!, $0.baseAddress!, n * ...) }` force-unwraps `$0.baseAddress!` *before* checking `if n > 0` (`:146`); an empty `verts` array crashes immediately.
- **Recommended change:** Guard `if n > 0` before `memcpy`; skip `recordPanel` when `game.hideHUD && game.menu == nil` (or `scopeHUDShown <= 0.01`); double-buffer `HudPanel.image` (or only re-record `HudPanel` when HUD state/vertices change).
- **Expected effect:** Eliminates a cross-frame GPU pipeline stall and a redundant offscreen render pass on most frames (plus fixes a latent crash).
- **Risk:** Very low; double-buffering `image` costs 2.6 MB VRAM.

### 9. Roomscale stepping walks straight through ship/train walls + broken ship `wallFade`
- **Evidence:**
  1. `QuestControls.roomScale` (`quest/src/app/QuestControls.swift:1325-1326`): `if pl.flying || game.riding != nil || game.world.ships.aboard != nil { if game.riding == nil { pos += delta } }`. Physically taking a step in your room while aboard a frigate, crawler, sloop, or train skips all collision — letting players walk straight through bridge glass, bulkheads, and out into open air.
  2. `QuestControls.afterTick` (`:1011-1025`) tests `wallFade` via `w.block(hx, hy, hz)` in world coordinates even when `w.frame != nil`, so leaning your head into a ship wall does not fade to black (X-ray peering through ship hulls).
- **Recommended change:** When `ships.aboard != nil`, run roomscale `delta` through the ship's collision (`w.frame` / `moveBody` in ship space, matching stick walking on decks), and test `wallFade` against `ship.grid` at `ship.toLocal(hw)`.
- **Expected effect:** Stops players from accidentally stepping out of moving capital ships/trains in roomscale VR and closes an immersion-breaking X-ray glitch.
- **Risk:** Low; verify with `ridecheck` / boarding scenes that normal deck motion isn't snagged.

### 10. Store-facing polish & player-trust defects that invite refund/Early-Access criticism
- **Evidence (all visible to CA$13.99 Meta Horizon Store buyers):**
  1. **Permanent world deletion lies ("Moved to the Trash"):** `WorldStore.useTrash` defaults to `true` (`Sources/WorldStore.swift:6`). On Android, `trashItem` fails (`:100`) and falls back to `fm.removeItem` (`:101`) — **permanently deleting the world** — yet `PauseMenu.swift:249,848` says `"Move this world to the Trash."` and toasts `"Moved <name> to the Trash"`.
  2. **Synchronous "Copy World" VR freeze:** `PauseMenu.swift:839-842` calls `WorldStore.copy` (`WorldStore.swift:84-93`) synchronously on the main VR thread, freezing the headset display (0 fps) for seconds on explored worlds.
  3. **Mac path & old `"Blocksmith"` name leaked in Quest Options > Audio:** `AudioMenu.swift:17` leaves `audio_music_src` visible on Quest (`QuestOptions.swift:54` doesn't hide it), whose help text (`PauseMenu.swift:206`) reads: `"Built-in: the game's own music. My Music: your files from ~/Library/Application Support/Blocksmith/Music. Mixed: they take turns."`
  4. **Dev-only `"Test Mode (Creative on Death)"` exposed to buyers:** `PauseMenu.swift:235,377` renders `"Test Mode (Creative on Death)"` (help text: `"Testing: ..."`) in Options > Interface even when `devTools == false` (`Settings.swift:113`).
  5. **Developer placeholder world name on first launch:** `QuestApp.swift:134,195` names every buyer's first world `"Quest World"` (displayed on the opening loading screen as `SUNDERFALL / Quest World`).
  6. **First-Launch Setup permanently skipped if interrupted:** `QuestApp.swift:380-384` sets `QuestSettings.setupShown = true` as soon as the wizard opens rather than when `"Start Playing"` is pressed (`QuestOptions.swift:243` already sets it there).
  7. **Unused `XR_FB_color_space` extension (`XRSession.swift:175`):** Enabled at instance creation but `xrSetColorSpaceFB` is never called anywhere.
- **Recommended change:** Set `WorldStore.useTrash = false` on Android and update confirm text to warn of permanent deletion; move `WorldStore.copy` to a background queue; add Quest `hostHelp["audio_music_src"]` (`"Sunderfall: recorded soundtrack. Classic: synthesized score."`); gate `testmode` on `st.devTools || st.testMode`; rename default world to `"My World"` (or `"Sunderfall"`); remove eager `QuestSettings.setupShown = true` from `QuestApp.swift:381`; either call `xrSetColorSpaceFB` or drop the extension.
- **Expected effect:** Eliminates glaring Mac-port/dev leaks in the first 15 minutes, prevents accidental permanent save loss, and stops world-copy VR freezes.
- **Risk:** Very low.

## What Cannot Be Known Without Testing on the Headset

- **Exact AAudio hardware sample rate & latency on Quest 3:** Whether `AAudioStreamBuilder_setSampleRate(44100)` + `LOW_LATENCY` actually opens at 48000 Hz (pitching all audio +8.84% sharp) or falls back to AudioFlinger resampling can only be confirmed by reading `audio: AAudio stream started, <sr> Hz` in `blocksmith.log` on the headset, plus testing Bluetooth earbud disconnect/reconnect recovery.
- **Actual GPU/CPU savings of removing `CostProbe`, `DeviceClocks` sysfs reads, and the per-frame `HudPanel` barrier:** Adreno 740 tile-pass / pipeline-barrier timings and Qualcomm thermal-zone sysfs read latencies vary by driver/OS version and must be timed on device.
- **Meta Horizon OS soft keyboard (`ANativeActivity_showSoftInput`) behavior:** Whether Horizon OS ever surfaces a working keyboard or delivers `AKEY_EVENT_ACTION_DOWN` vs `MULTIPLE` to a pure `NativeActivity` OpenXR app must be checked on device.
- **Store-signed entitlement check under Airplane Mode & non-entitled accounts:** Whether `ovr_Entitlement_GetIsViewerEntitled` returns cached success offline or an error code (which `QuestEntitlement.swift:54` currently treats as denial) can only be verified with a real `STORE=1` release-signed APK on Quest 3.
- **Real-world Teleport feel, roomscale ship collision, and untethered 60+ minute thermals:** Host harnesses (`QuestSim`, `headset_gate`) drive synthetic pad input on USB power and do not exercise real Touch controller arcs, physical roomscale steps on moving ships, or battery-powered thermal throttling.
