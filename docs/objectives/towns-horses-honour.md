# Towns, horses, honour, frigate gun: full brief + expanded objectives (Remington + overseer, Oct 9-10)

SCOPE NOTE (updated Oct 9, 22:16): towns of people, shops and dollars (A2, A3) are already in claude/quest-port (merged PR #11). The cloud trial may still land the frigate gun (C, PR #12) and the military base look (B, PR #13). Before starting, run gh pr view 12 and 13: skip whichever is MERGED, and build the rest yourself here. Do A1 (horses), A4 and D (remaining Halo renames) in full.

Remington's direction, Oct 9 PM. These are ADDITIONS to all queued work.

A. Red Dead Redemption 2 as an inspiration for systems (borrow the FEEL and the mechanics; never names, characters, art, music or level layouts):
 1. Horses, everything: calling/whistle (exists, so check it's reliable at range and the horse pathfinds to you), bonding levels that unlock things (tighter turning, calmer under fire, tricks), brushing/feeding/patting, the horse remembering you, a stable to keep and swap horses, saddlebags as storage, horse stamina and health with care, gaits that feel weighty, smooth on slopes (see playtest task). Make the horse the player's companion.
 2. Villages become towns of PEOPLE, not villagers: humans with names, daily routines (work, eat, sleep), armed with swords, pitchforks and tools; they defend the town against mobs and react to the player (greet, warn, fight back if attacked). Remove Minecraft-style villager trading as the main economy.
 3. Shops in towns, several types like RDR2's general store, gunsmith, butcher, doctor, stable, tailor, blacksmith, saloon: buy and sell with a currency, plus a shop UI that's readable in VR. Prices balanced against progression (docs/STORE_QUALITY.md progression objective). Keep treasure maps and the current inventory (Remington is happy with them).
 4. Other RDR2-feel features worth it in this game: your call, but list what you added and why (e.g. camps, bounties/wanted level, hunting with pelt quality, fishing, ambient world events). Don't bloat it. Pick the few that make the world feel alive.

B. Military base art direction: inspired by the Siberia levels of GoldenEye 007 (N64, Facility/Bunker feel): grey concrete corridors, small blue lights in the corners, cold clean futuristic look, metal doors, the base under snow. Inspiration only: original layouts and assets.

C. The frigate (Meridian frigate, already exists with a MAC gun): the ship is built around the big spinal gun, so make the gun the centrepiece. A visible barrel along the hull, a charge-up (sound, glow, camera shake, VR haptics), and a shot that is DEVASTATING: huge impact, big but performance-bounded crater, shockwave knocking mobs flat, long rumble, slow reload. Measure frame time during the blast on Quest; it must not break the perf gate.

D. Store/IP: "UNSC" and "MAC" are Halo (Microsoft) terms and appear in player-visible text. Rename to our own faction and weapon names (internal code names can stay), and add them to docs/status/ip-renames.md. Same rule for anything RDR2/GoldenEye-specific. Not legal advice; flag unsure ones for Remington.

Standard rules: plan first (write docs/proposals/rdr-features.md with scope and order), build in commits, an automated check per feature, an independent verifier, update the scorecard. If it's too big for one task, finish A1 + A2 + A3 + C first and create a follow-up task file for the rest. Ship a quest-dist build at the end.

HONOUR (added 10-10 13:57 by Remington, do this FIRST in this task): add an honour level (RDR2-style) saved per world, shown subtly in the HUD/inventory. Stealing from villagers or their chests loses honour (bigger loss if seen); fair trades, helping and protecting villagers slowly regain it. When honour is low, villages are automatically hostile: villagers flee or warn, the sheriff and armed villagers attack on sight. Sheriff weapon: about a 5% chance per sheriff of carrying a gun (an existing Capital gun or a frontier variant of it); otherwise the normal weapon. Also: Remington saw lots of errors in villages in the latest Quest build (0.95). Walk through several generated villages on Mac and in the Quest build checks, find and fix the village bugs (broken buildings, pathing, floating/stuck villagers, missing blocks, crashes), and list what you fixed in the status doc.

EXPANDED OBJECTIVES (overseer, 10-10 14:45; meet these as well as everything above):
HONOUR: a subtle meter that pulses with a sound when honour changes and shows the reason; 5 tiers with real consequences (shop prices, greetings, townsfolk tips and gifts at high honour; refused service, posters and bounty hunters at very low honour); a redemption path (jobs, donations, protecting towns); per-world save with migration; honour actions logged so testing can prove each rule.
HORSES: name your horse (VR-friendly naming); breeds with different speed, stamina, courage and looks; taming wild horses with a calm-down minigame; horse fears predators and gunfire until bonded; horse armour; injured horse can be revived with a reviver item, death is permanent only if left; stable UI to buy, sell, store and swap; saddlebags reachable in VR by reaching to the horse's side; VR comfort while riding (vignette, smooth vs snap turn, a horizon-locked option); hooves sound per surface; the horse waits, grazes and wanders a little when left.
TOWNS: every town has a name on a sign and the map; a notice board with bounties and jobs; a doctor who heals and sells tonics; a bank for the emerald exchange; gunsmith upgrades; a saloon with one simple game (cards or dice); street lamps at night; town defence events (raids at night) where townsfolk fight beside you; people remember you (greet by name at high honour); shopkeepers restock over days; at least 3 town layouts per biome style.
FRIGATE GUN: a gunner seat or console with an aiming reticle, charge meter and cooldown display; targets worth hitting (enemy ships, the Capital and military bases); damage interacts with structures sensibly; a camera-shake comfort toggle for VR; the shot recorded on video-like screenshot sequence as evidence.
MILITARY BASE LOOK: snow outside, cold blue corner lights, concrete corridors, metal doors with sounds, alarms with red lights during combat, interior rooms with purpose (barracks, comms, armoury) and loot.
IP: a grep-based test that fails if UNSC, MAC or other flagged names appear in player-visible strings; ip-renames.md complete.
VILLAGE BUGS: write each bug found with seed and coordinates and a regression test where possible.
