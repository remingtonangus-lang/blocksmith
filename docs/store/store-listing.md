# Store listing (draft)

## Name
Blocksmith

## Short description (≤ 140 characters)
Build, ride and fight across a living frontier: dig your own world, run a town's law, ride horses and sail warships in VR.

## Long description
Blocksmith is a block-built frontier sandbox made for VR. Every hill can be dug and every wall can be built. The world
around you is alive: towns with shopkeepers and lawmen, outlaw camps, rival military forces, a navy on the coast and a
war you can join or end.

- **Dig and build with your hands.** Swing a pickaxe, place blocks, craft tools and gear, and shape the world one block at
  a time. Choose block terrain, or smooth polygon terrain with the same digging underneath.
- **Ride and sail.** Saddle a horse and gallop between towns, take the helm of a frigate, or ride a siege carriage
  into battle.
- **Real VR gunplay.** Revolvers, rifles, shotguns and heavy guns, each with its own reload by hand: pull the magazine
  from your hip, seat it, work the action. Brace a rifle with both hands and look down the scope.
- **Towns with law and money.** Take contracts, trade emeralds for cash at the bank, buy from the gunsmith. Rob a train if
  you dare and see how the town answers: wanted posters, bounty hunters and a jail cell.
- **A war to win.** Take a Greywall base, break the Capital's grip on the coast, descend into Emberdeep and face the
  Blight.
- **Play together.** Invite a friend on your Wi-Fi to share your world, your horses, your ships and your robberies.

Seated, standing and reclined play. Comfort options for snap or smooth turning, a vignette and left-handed controls.

## Key features (bullets for the store page)
- Hand-built VR mining, crafting and building in an endless world
- Horses, frigates, airships and vehicles you ride and crew
- Physical VR weapons with hands-on reloads
- Living frontier towns: shops, contracts, law, bounties and jail
- A campaign through military bases, the navy and the underworld
- Two-player Play Together over local Wi-Fi
