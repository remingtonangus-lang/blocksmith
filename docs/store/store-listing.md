# Store listing (draft)

## Name
Sunderfall

## Short description (≤ 140 characters)
Build, ride and fight across a living frontier: dig your own world, run a town's law, ride horses and sail warships in VR.

## Long description
Sunderfall is a frontier sandbox made for VR. The land is real ground, not cubes: smooth hills, round trees, fallen
logs and deep caves, and every bit of it can be dug, blasted and built on. The world around you is alive: towns with
shopkeepers and lawmen, homesteads and ghost towns, outlaw gangs, rival military forces, a navy on the coast and a war
you can join or end.

- **Dig and build with your hands.** Swing a pickaxe and the ground breaks where it strikes; blasts and bullets chip
  pieces off. Craft tools and gear, build homes, forts and workshops.
- **Ride and sail.** Bond with a horse and gallop between towns, ride the train (or its roof), take the helm of a
  frigate, or build a tank at the Vehicle Works and drive it into battle.
- **Hunt the wild.** Herds of deer, elk and bison; read the wind, follow tracks and blood trails, set bait and snares,
  and track down legendary animals.
- **Real VR gunplay.** Revolvers, rifles, shotguns and heavy guns, each with its own reload by hand: pull the magazine
  from your hip, seat it, work the action. Brace a rifle with both hands and look down the scope.
- **Towns with law and money.** Take contracts, hunt gang leaders for their bounties, buy a homestead, trade at the
  bank and the gunsmith. Rob a train (or the bullion train) if you dare and see how the town answers: wanted posters,
  bounty hunters and a jail cell.
- **A war to win.** Take a Greywall base, break the Capital's grip on the coast, descend into Emberdeep and face the
  Blight.
- **Play together.** Split screen, or a friend on your Wi-Fi: share your world, ride double, man the guns while your
  partner drives, split the pay and pick each other up when one of you goes down.

Seated, standing and reclined play. Comfort options for snap or smooth turning, a vignette and left-handed controls.

## Key features (bullets for the store page)
- Hand-built VR mining, crafting and building in an endless, smooth, breakable world
- Horses, frigates, airships and vehicles you ride and crew
- Physical VR weapons with hands-on reloads
- Living frontier towns: shops, contracts, law, bounties and jail; homesteads, ghost towns and pueblos out on the land
- Hunting with wind, tracks, bait and snares
- A campaign through military bases, the navy and the underworld
- Two-player co-op: split screen or Play Together over local Wi-Fi, with gun seats and revives
