## What Sunderfall collects

No data leaves your headset. Sunderfall has no account, no multiplayer, no analytics, no advertising and no telemetry,
and it never contacts a server on the internet.

## What stays on your headset

- **Worlds and settings:** your worlds, inventory, options and the rolling backup copies of each world are stored in the
  app's private storage on the headset. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- **Crash marker and logs:** a small marker that tells the game whether the last session ended normally, plus a short
  crash and stutter log (time, frame timings, which game system was slow). These files stay on the headset.
- **Microphone:** Sunderfall does not use the microphone. The store build has no recording code and does not ask for
  the microphone permission.

## The Director (after the story)

Once the story is finished, the Director plans new events for your world. Its rules run entirely on your headset and
send nothing anywhere.

## Platform services

If the store build checks that you own the app, it uses the headset platform's own entitlement service. That service
is covered by the platform holder's privacy policy, and Sunderfall receives only a yes or no answer.

## Children

Sunderfall does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with the update that changes it.
