## What Sunderfall collects

Nothing leaves your headset unless you send a report. Sunderfall has no account, no multiplayer, no analytics, no
advertising and no telemetry.

## Bug and idea reports (only when you send one)

The pause menu's **Report a Bug or Idea** screen sends what you type there to the developer, only when you press Send.
A report holds:

- **Your words** (up to 2,000 characters) and whether it is a bug or an idea. Please don't type personal information.
- **A report ID:** a random code made when the app is installed (shown on the report screen). It is not linked to your
  account, your name or your headset; reinstalling the app makes a new one. It lets the developer tell one player's
  reports apart and limit how many a player can send (5 a day).
- **Game details:** the game's version, where you were in your world (position, direction, biome, the world's seed),
  what you held and were looking at, the game mode, health, time of day, weather and frame rate.

Reports go over HTTPS to a Google Apps Script web app run by the developer, which emails each one to the developer and
keeps it in a private spreadsheet in the developer's Google account (Google's privacy policy covers that service). They
are used only to fix bugs and plan the game, never sold or shared, and kept until they are no longer needed. To have
your reports deleted, email the contact address above with the report ID shown on the report screen.
A report that cannot be sent (no connection) waits on the headset, at most 5, and goes the next time you open the
report screen.

## What stays on your headset

- **Worlds and settings:** your worlds, inventory, options and the rolling backup copies of each world are stored in the
  app's private storage on the headset. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- **Crash marker and logs:** a small marker that tells the game whether the last session ended normally, plus a short
  crash and stutter log (time, frame timings, which game system was slow). These files stay on the headset.
- **Microphone:** Sunderfall does not use the microphone. The store build has no recording code and does not ask for
  the microphone permission.

## The Director (after the story)

Once the story is finished, the Director plans new events for your world. Its rules run entirely on your headset and
send nothing anywhere.

## Platform services

If the store build checks that you own the app, it uses the headset platform's own entitlement service. That service
is covered by the platform holder's privacy policy, and Sunderfall receives only a yes or no answer.

## Children

Sunderfall does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with the update that changes it.
