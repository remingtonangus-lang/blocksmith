## What Blocksmith collects

Nothing leaves your headset unless you start Play Together. Blocksmith has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no
telemetry, and it never contacts a server run by the publisher.

## What stays on your headset

- **Worlds and settings:** your worlds, inventory, options and the rolling backup copies of each world are stored in the
  app's private storage on the headset. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
- **Crash marker and logs:** a small marker that tells the game whether the last session ended normally, plus a short
  crash and stutter log (time, frame timings, which game system was slow). These files stay on the headset.
- **Microphone:** the store version does not use the microphone. Development and playtest builds can record spoken bug
  notes, and the store build has none of that code or the permission.

## Play Together (local network)

When you host or join a Play Together session, the game connects directly to the other headset over your local Wi-Fi.
Each headset sends the other its player name, position, actions and the world data needed to play. Hosting also
announces the game on the local network so friends can find it. Nothing goes over the internet or to the publisher.
This data is not stored after the session ends, except that edits to the host's world are saved in the host's world.

## Platform services

If the store build checks that you own the app, it uses the headset platform's own entitlement service. That service
is covered by the platform holder's privacy policy, and Blocksmith receives only a yes or no answer.

## Children

Blocksmith does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published with the update that changes it.
