# Store media candidates, Oct 31 (none final until Remington picks)

Rendered in the cloud by the game's own Quest renderer (questcheck --cine, branch claude/project-thread-1q6065), his
world seed 7639458412018979794. No other game's names, logos or looks; no HUD.

- sunderfall_trailer_captions.mp4: 60 s, 1920x1080, 30 fps, AAC. 13 shots: frontier village, its street, the Capital,
  a passenger train, a cabin built block by block, a Boreal Station at night (air, gate, corridor), an Ashguard
  advance against Capital riflemen, a tank knocked out, a frigate, its main gun striking the village tower, sunset,
  title card. Short captions. Music: main_title into ash_vault_battle, with the game's blast sounds.
- sunderfall_trailer_clean.mp4: the same without captions.
- screenshots/: 13 action stills, 2560x1440 (drawn at 5120x2880, scaled down).
- clips/: each shot on its own (1920x1080) for re-editing.

Known: far structures show as pale stand-in boxes over the fog; the battle clip is cut before a smoke puff fills the
lens; the strike is filmed low because a fresh crater showed the sky through its floor in these renders.

On this branch the trailers are re-encoded (CRF 19, ~72 MB each) to fit GitHub's 100 MB file limit; the full-quality 112 MB masters are in the project files.
