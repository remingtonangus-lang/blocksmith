# Frontier — world bible (working)

Working title **Sable River** (codename Frontier). Fictional frontier territory, autumn 1899. Everything here is
original: setting, geography, people, story.

## The land: the Sable River country (8 × 8 km playable map, 2 m heightmap)
North is −Z. Map coordinates run −4096..4096 m on X (east) and Z (south). Lake Agnes sits at 120 m.

| Region | Where | Character |
|---|---|---|
| Kestrel Range | north-west | Granite and pine mountains to ~1550 m, snow above ~1150 m, high meadows, mining camps |
| Thornwood | north-east | Rolling pine and aspen hills, logging, lakes, homesteads |
| Sable River valley | centre | Cottonwood river bottoms, ranches, the main town and the railroad crossing |
| Corrigan Plains | south-centre | Open shortgrass prairie, cattle ranches, windmills, pronghorn and bison |
| Ocotillo Breaks | south-west | Red-rock mesas, buttes and slot canyons, scrub desert, an old mission |
| Linden Shore / Agnes Marsh | east | Wetland delta, the lake, the port town with brick buildings |

## Settlements
- **Bitter Spring** — river crossing and railroad town (saloon, hotel, general store, gunsmith, sheriff, bank,
  doctor, barber, stable, church, depot). Story hub.
- **Coldwater** — muddy silver-mining camp in a high valley of the Kestrel Range.
- **Mesquite Wells** — desert water stop on the rail line; adobe and timber.
- **Port Linden** — lakeside port and the territory's only brick town; money, newspapers, the railroad office.
- Ranches, homesteads, a logging camp, trading post, mission ruin, trapper cabins.

## Story premise (outline; full beats in design/STORY.md)
Ruth Caddell — 34, once the sharpshooter "Miss Ruthie" of a travelling show, later a county deputy — comes to the
Sable River country hunting the men who shot her husband in a payroll robbery. She finds a territory being bought
out from under its people by the Meridian & Western land syndicate, a drought, and an outfit of strays she ends up
leading: a wounded drover, a disgraced doctor, a cardsharp, a Lakota-Irish scout, a runaway kid. Her choices on the
trail (Standing) decide whether she leaves as a lawman, an outlaw, or neither.

Mechanic names: **Nerve** (slow-time aiming), **Standing** (honour), the **Outfit** (camp).
