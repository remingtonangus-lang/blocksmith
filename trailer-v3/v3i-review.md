# Sunderfall trailer v3i: blind review (Nov 14, late morning)

Cut: `Sunderfall trailer v3i Nov 14.mp4` (40.3 s, 1080p, 11 shots, captions). It was rendered on quest-port after PR #734 and is the best cut so far.

There have been nine cuts since last night (v3a to v3i). After each one, a fresh reviewer agent watched it blind with the video-review skill. **The verdict is still NOT YET.**

## Fixed along the way

- WWII-looking uniforms and black outlaw dress.
- The grey controller block: it is now gloved hands, and the building hand lasers each block it places.
- The ride is first person on the horse.
- The frigate gun is shot from the deck, so the ship is no longer scorched by its own blast.
- The town is now the timber village.
- Smaller fixes: the farm "blade", tracer streaks, the townsman walking into the lens, the stray aim line, and the villager at the frame edge.

## Still open in v3i (most noticeable first)

1. **It doesn't read as VR.** It is flat capture, the hands don't grab, and the left fist sits at the frame edge. Every review has raised this. The one real fix is mixed-reality or headset footage, or showing a player wearing the headset.
2. **The muzzle flash is a flat yellow sprite with holes.** At the launcher shot it covers the middle of the frame. This is the game's own flash art.
3. **Staged outlaws stand idle while they are shot.** When they fire back, their tracers streak across the lens.
4. **Building and farming look scripted.** Several blocks appear at once, and nothing gets farmed.
5. **There is no story thread, and the snow base never appears.**
6. **Smaller points:**
   - The train has no steam, and a man stands on the rails as it arrives.
   - The cave is murky and the torch is unlit.
   - The horse shows only ears and neck.
   - The frigate fires at empty forest.
   - Sunflowers show a seam down the middle.

Clean: captions, the end card, audio levels, and no real-world insignia.
