# Sunderfall trailer v3m: blind review (Nov 14, afternoon)

Cut: `Sunderfall trailer v3m Nov 14.mp4` (42.2 s, 1080p, 12 shots, captions). It was rendered on quest-port after PR #738.

**Verdict: NOT YET.** This is the backup cut. The real trailer will be cut from Remington's next full-resolution headset clips.

## Fixed since v3l
- The muzzle flash now sits at the barrel. It is gone from the findings.
- No one walks on the rails in front of the train.
- No sheep at the edge of the finale.
- The cave and torch read well, and the horse ride is fine.

## Still open (most noticeable first)
1. **It doesn't read as VR.** There is no grabbing or reaching, and the second hand sits at the frame edge. Real headset footage is the fix.
2. **The locomotive looks broken up close.** The boiler stands off the frame, the engine overhangs the curve, and the chimney gives no steam.
3. **A burning soldier stands idle inside the wrecked tank.**
4. **"Work the land" shows nothing being worked.**
5. **The enemies never react.** The corridor soldiers have their backs turned, and the outlaws stand in a line.
6. **The base's olive and black look may read as WWII to a reviewer.** No real-world symbols were found.
7. **Building:** pieces pop in several at once.
8. **Frigate:** it fires at empty forest, the boom comes 0.5 s early, and the side-on shot is static.
9. **Smaller points:**
   - Dots float above the opening ship.
   - The sunflowers show a seam.
   - The launcher reads as a dark lump.
   - The gunshots are quiet.
   - There is no story thread.

**Clean:** the cave, the horse ride, the village close, the captions, the end card, and no other game's IP.
