# Sunderfall trailer r1 (your Nov 14 headset clips): blind review

The cut is `Sunderfall trailer r1 Nov 14 (your headset clips).mp4`: 49 s, 14 shots, all from today's play.

**Verdict: NOT YET.**

## The one thing only a new recording fixes

**The HUD is in every shot.** The reviewer read it as debug output on top of the trailer:
- the minimap, coordinates and biome name
- "Your things 5250 m", "Honour", "$16.00" and "LOADED 200"
- world notes, the hotbar, and the pointer line

It covers the subject: the minimap sits on the house as it blows up. In the sunset, the back of a panel and mirrored text float in the sky.

The fix is to play the trailer shots with **Hide HUD** on (pause menu > Settings) and Coordinates & Details off. I haven't checked yet whether that also hides the floating notes and the minimap in VR.

## Fixed in r2 (cut from the same clips)

- **Tree felling** is cut. The upper trunk hangs in the air, then the tree stands again; that's a game bug, so I'm passing it to the bug hunt.
- **Fort fight** is cut. The white trooper with a black visor reads like a famous film's soldiers.
- **The soldier lines in the Ash Vault** are cut. Field-grey soldiers, a black and red tank and striped barriers read as WWII German. The German-sounding gun names on the HUD ("Mahlwerk", "Himmelsturm") add to it.
- **Captions** now match what's on screen:
  - "Face giant war machines" (it has wheels; it doesn't walk, and nobody pilots it)
  - "Explore the caves below" (nobody digs)
  - "Build block by block"
  - "Or blow it all up"

## Still open in r2

- **HUD and pointer line:** this needs a new recording, as above.
- **It doesn't read as VR.** No hands are visible. The tank shot is the third-person chase view, and the fast flying camera rolls a lot.
- **The beach under the build** has a dark grey stain on the sand.
- **The stilt hamlet note** floats in the world, and the hamlet is flat red.
- **The coast horizon** is a pale fog band.
