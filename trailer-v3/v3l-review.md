# Sunderfall trailer v3l: blind review (Nov 14, midday)

Cut: `Sunderfall trailer v3l Nov 14.mp4` (42.2 s, 1080p, 12 shots, captions). It was rendered on quest-port after PR #736.

**Verdict: NOT YET.** Even so, this is a backup cut; the real trailer will be cut from Remington's next full-resolution headset clips.

## Fixed since v3i (Remington's notes)

- **Cave torch.** The torch is now held upright by its stick in the fist, and its flame is lit. The reviewer calls the cave clean.
- **Frigate.** A new shot shows the whole ship side-on in the sky before the deck-gun shot.

## Still open in v3l (most noticeable first)

1. **Muzzle flash.** It sits beside the hand, not at the end of the rifle. A fix is built for v3m: the flash now goes at the drawn gun's muzzle.
2. **Outlaws.** They stand idle while they are shot, and there is no town in the shot.
3. **Corridor soldier.** He is hit and doesn't react.
4. **Train.** A man walks on the rails in front of it. v3m clears him.
5. **Building and farming.** Building shows several blocks appearing at once, and in the farm shot nothing gets farmed.
6. **Story.** There is no story thread, and the snow base and the war below are missing.
7. **VR read.** It doesn't read as VR (a flat view, with the second hand at the frame edge). Real headset footage is the fix.
8. **Smaller points:**
   - The horse shows only its ears.
   - The frigate fires at empty jungle, and its boom comes 0.5 s early.
   - Dots float above the opening ship.
   - The sunflowers show a seam.
   - A sheep's head pokes in at the finale's edge (v3m clears it).

**Clean:** the cave, the captions, the end card, and no real-world insignia.
